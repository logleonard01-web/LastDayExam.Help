Corrections Policy

We want our content to be accurate. If you find a factual error, an incorrect answer or an unclear explanation, tell us using the details on the contact page, including the page address and what you believe is wrong.

We review reports, correct confirmed errors promptly and, where an error could have misled readers, note the correction on the page. We do not change publication dates to make old content look new.
