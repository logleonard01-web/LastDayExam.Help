A

  • AccelerationThe rate of change of velocity.Physics
  • AcidA substance that produces hydrogen ions in water and has a pH below 7.Chemistry
  • Affect vs effectAffect is usually a verb meaning to influence; effect is usually a noun meaning a result.English
  • AlgorithmA finite, ordered set of clear steps for solving a problem.Computer science
  • AnalyzeBreak something into parts and examine how those parts relate to each other.Command words
  • AreaThe amount of surface a 2D shape covers.Mathematics
  • ArgumentA claim supported by reasons and evidence.English
  • AssessJudge the importance, quality or extent of something, weighing evidence.Command words

B

  • BaseA substance that neutralises acids.Chemistry
  • BiasA tendency to present or favour one point of view unfairly.Research
  • BooleanA data type with two possible values, true or false.Computer science

C

  • CalculateWork out a numerical answer, showing the steps.Command words
  • CausationA relationship where one event or factor brings about another.Critical thinking
  • CellThe basic unit of structure and function in living organisms.Biology
  • Chemical equationA representation of a chemical reaction using formulas.Chemistry
  • ChlorophyllThe green pigment in plants that absorbs light for photosynthesis.Biology
  • ChloroplastAn organelle in plant and algal cells that contains chlorophyll and carries out photosynthesis.Biology
  • CitationA reference in your text that shows where an idea, quotation or fact came from.Research
  • ClassifySort items into groups according to shared features.Command words
  • CoefficientA number that multiplies a variable.Mathematics
  • CompareDescribe similarities and differences between two or more things.Command words
  • Confounding variableA third factor that influences both of the variables being studied and creates a misleading link.Critical thinking
  • Consumer price indexA measure of the average change in prices of a basket of goods and services bought by households.Economics
  • ContrastFocus on the differences between two or more things.Command words
  • CorrelationA relationship in which two variables tend to change together.Critical thinking
  • CredibilityThe degree to which a source can be trusted.Research
  • CritiqueGive a reasoned judgment of the strengths and weaknesses of something.Command words

D

  • Data typeThe kind of value a variable holds, such as integer, string or Boolean.Computer science
  • DefineState the precise meaning of a term.Command words
  • DemandThe quantity of a good that buyers are willing and able to buy at each price.Economics
  • DemonstrateShow how something works or is true, using evidence, examples or steps.Command words
  • Dependent variableThe variable you measure in an experiment.Scientific method
  • DeriveObtain a result or formula from earlier results through logical steps.Command words
  • DescribeGive an account of the main features of something.Command words
  • DetermineEstablish an answer by calculation or from evidence.Command words
  • DiscussConsider several points of view or factors, with reasoning.Command words
  • DisplacementThe straight-line distance and direction from the start to the end point.Physics
  • DistinguishShow the difference between two or more things.Command words

E

  • ElaborateAdd detail, explanation or examples to a point.Command words
  • EquationA statement that two expressions are equal.Mathematics
  • EquatorThe line of latitude at 0 degrees that circles Earth midway between the poles.History and geography
  • EquilibriumThe point where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.Economics
  • EstimateGive an approximate value using reasonable assumptions.Command words
  • EvaluateWeigh strengths and limitations and reach a supported judgment.Command words
  • EventA set of one or more outcomes in probability.Mathematics
  • EvidenceFacts, data, examples or sources used to support a claim.Research
  • ExamineLook closely at something and investigate its parts or details.Command words
  • ExplainGive reasons for how or why something happens.Command words
  • ExpressionA combination of numbers, variables and operations without an equals sign.Mathematics

F

  • FactorA number or expression that divides another exactly.Mathematics
  • FallacyAn error in reasoning that weakens an argument.Critical thinking
  • FlowchartA diagram that shows the steps of a process using shapes and arrows.Computer science
  • ForceA push or pull on an object, measured in newtons.Physics
  • FractionA number that represents part of a whole, written as one number over another.Mathematics

G

  • GlucoseA simple sugar that cells use as a source of energy.Biology

H

  • HypotenuseThe longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle.Mathematics
  • HypothesisA testable explanation or prediction that evidence can support or contradict.Scientific method

I

  • IdentifyName or pick out something from information provided.Command words
  • IllustrateMake a point clear by giving examples or a diagram.Command words
  • Independent variableThe variable you deliberately change in an experiment.Scientific method
  • InequalityA statement that compares two expressions using less than, greater than or similar signs.Mathematics
  • InertiaThe tendency of an object to resist changes in its motion.Physics
  • InferDraw a conclusion from evidence or reasoning rather than from a direct statement.Command words
  • InflationA sustained rise in the general level of prices.Economics
  • IntegerA whole number, positive, negative or zero.Computer science
  • Interest rateThe price of borrowing money, or the return on saving it, expressed as a percentage.Economics
  • InterpretExplain the meaning or significance of information such as data, text or a graph.Command words
  • IterationThe repetition of a set of instructions.Computer science
  • Its vs it'sIts shows possession; it's means it is or it has.English

J

  • JustifySupport a decision or conclusion with reasons or evidence.Command words

L

  • LatitudeThe angle of a place north or south of the equator, in degrees.History and geography
  • LongitudeThe angle of a place east or west of the prime meridian, in degrees.History and geography

M

  • MassThe amount of matter in an object, measured in kilograms.Physics
  • MeanThe sum of all values divided by the number of values.Mathematics
  • MedianThe middle value when data is arranged in order.Mathematics
  • MetaphorA figure of speech that describes one thing as if it were another, without like or as.English
  • MitochondrionAn organelle where aerobic respiration releases energy for the cell.Biology
  • ModeThe most frequent value in a data set.Mathematics

N

  • NeutralisationThe reaction of an acid with a base to form a salt and water.Chemistry

O

  • Opportunity costThe value of the next best alternative given up when a choice is made.Economics
  • OrganelleA structure inside a cell that has a specific function.Biology
  • OutcomeOne possible result of a probability experiment.Mathematics
  • OutlineGive the main points or steps in brief.Command words

P

  • ParaphraseRestate an idea from a source in your own words and sentence structure.Research
  • PercentageA fraction expressed out of 100.Mathematics
  • PerimeterThe total distance around the outside of a 2D shape.Mathematics
  • pHA scale, usually from 0 to 14, that measures how acidic or alkaline a solution is.Chemistry
  • PhotosynthesisThe process by which plants and some other organisms use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water.Biology
  • PlagiarismPresenting someone else's words or ideas as your own.Research
  • PredictState what you expect to happen, based on knowledge or evidence.Command words
  • Primary sourceA first-hand record created at the time of an event or by someone who experienced it.History and geography
  • ProbabilityA number from 0 to 1 that measures how likely an event is.Mathematics
  • ProductA substance formed in a chemical reaction.Chemistry
  • ProveShow with logical steps that a statement is true.Command words
  • PseudocodeA structured way to describe an algorithm that looks like code but is not tied to any language.Computer science

Q

  • Quadratic equationAn equation that can be written as ax squared plus bx plus c equals 0, where a is not zero.Mathematics

R

  • RadiusThe distance from the centre of a circle to its edge.Mathematics
  • RangeThe difference between the largest and smallest values.Mathematics
  • RatioA comparison of two quantities showing how many times one contains the other.Mathematics
  • ReactantA substance that is present at the start of a chemical reaction and is changed by it.Chemistry
  • Right angleAn angle of exactly 90 degrees.Mathematics
  • RootA solution of an equation, or a number that when multiplied by itself a given number of times gives a value.Mathematics

S

  • ScarcityThe condition that resources are limited while wants are unlimited.Economics
  • Secondary sourceA source created later that analyses or interprets primary sources.History and geography
  • SimileA figure of speech that compares two things using like or as.English
  • StateGive a brief, clear answer without explanation.Command words
  • StringA sequence of characters, such as text.Computer science
  • SuggestPut forward a possible idea or explanation, usually based on given information.Command words
  • SummarizeGive the main points briefly.Command words
  • SupplyThe quantity of a good that sellers are willing and able to sell at each price.Economics

T

  • Their, there, they'reTheir shows possession, there shows place or existence, and they're means they are.English
  • Then vs thanThen refers to time or sequence; than is used in comparisons.English
  • Thesis statementA sentence that states the central, arguable claim of an essay.English
  • Topic sentenceA sentence that states the main idea of a paragraph.English
  • Trade-offA situation where gaining more of one thing means having less of another.Economics

V

  • VariableA symbol, often a letter, representing a quantity that can change or is unknown.Mathematics
  • Variable (programming)A named location in memory that stores a value which can change.Computer science
  • VelocitySpeed in a given direction.Physics
  • VolumeThe amount of space inside a 3D object.Mathematics
