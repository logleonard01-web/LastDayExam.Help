Chlorophyll

noun · Biology

Definition

The green pigment in plants that absorbs light for photosynthesis.

Explanation

It is found in chloroplasts and absorbs mainly red and blue light, reflecting green.

Example

Leaves look green because of chlorophyll.

Common confusion

Chlorophyll is a pigment; the chloroplast is the organelle that contains it.

Related terms

  • PhotosynthesisThe process by which plants and some other organisms use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water.
  • ChloroplastAn organelle in plant and algal cells that contains chlorophyll and carries out photosynthesis.
