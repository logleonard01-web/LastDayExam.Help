Coefficient

noun · Mathematics

Definition

A number that multiplies a variable.

Explanation

In 7x, the coefficient of x is 7. In chemistry, a coefficient is the number in front of a formula in a balanced equation.

Example

In 3x + 5, the coefficient of x is 3.

Common confusion

A coefficient is the multiplier, not the constant term.

Related terms

  • VariableA symbol, often a letter, representing a quantity that can change or is unknown.
  • ExpressionA combination of numbers, variables and operations without an equals sign.
