Inflation

noun · Economics

Definition

A sustained rise in the general level of prices.

Explanation

It reduces the purchasing power of money. It is usually measured by changes in a price index.

Example

If the price index rises 5% over a year, inflation is 5%.

Common confusion

When inflation falls, prices are still rising, only more slowly.

Related terms

  • Consumer price indexA measure of the average change in prices of a basket of goods and services bought by households.
  • Interest rateThe price of borrowing money, or the return on saving it, expressed as a percentage.
