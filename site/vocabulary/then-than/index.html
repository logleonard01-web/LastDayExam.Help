Then vs than

noun · English

Definition

Then refers to time or sequence; than is used in comparisons.

Explanation

If you are comparing, use than.

Example

She is taller than him. First we ate, then we left.

Common confusion

Mixing them up is very common in quick writing.

Related terms

  • Affect vs effectAffect is usually a verb meaning to influence; effect is usually a noun meaning a result.
