Area

noun · Mathematics

Definition

The amount of surface a 2D shape covers.

Explanation

Measured in square units such as cm squared. Different shapes have different formulas.

Example

A rectangle 4 cm by 3 cm has area 12 cm squared.

Common confusion

Area is not the same as perimeter.

Related terms

  • PerimeterThe total distance around the outside of a 2D shape.
  • VolumeThe amount of space inside a 3D object.
