Inequality

noun · Mathematics

Definition

A statement that compares two expressions using less than, greater than or similar signs.

Explanation

Solutions are often a range of values. Reverse the sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.

Example

2x + 1 < 7 gives x < 3.

Common confusion

An inequality has many solutions; most equations have a limited number.

Related terms

  • EquationA statement that two expressions are equal.
  • VariableA symbol, often a letter, representing a quantity that can change or is unknown.
