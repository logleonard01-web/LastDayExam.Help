Its vs it's

noun · English

Definition

Its shows possession; it's means it is or it has.

Explanation

The apostrophe in it's shows a missing letter, not possession.

Example

It's clear that the dog wagged its tail.

Common confusion

Unlike nouns, the possessive its has no apostrophe.

Related terms

  • Their, there, they'reTheir shows possession, there shows place or existence, and they're means they are.
