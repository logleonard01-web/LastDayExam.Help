Range

noun · Mathematics

Definition

The difference between the largest and smallest values.

Explanation

The range is a simple measure of spread. It is affected by extreme values.

Example

For 4, 7, 7, 9, 13 the range is 9.

Common confusion

Range is one number, not the pair of smallest and largest values.

Related terms

  • MeanThe sum of all values divided by the number of values.
  • MedianThe middle value when data is arranged in order.
