Dependent variable

noun · Scientific method

Definition

The variable you measure in an experiment.

Explanation

It depends on the independent variable. It is plotted on the y-axis of a graph.

Example

The rate of bubble production from pondweed.

Common confusion

The dependent variable is measured, not changed.

Related terms

  • Independent variableThe variable you deliberately change in an experiment.
  • HypothesisA testable explanation or prediction that evidence can support or contradict.
