Discuss

verb · Command words

Definition

Consider several points of view or factors, with reasoning.

Explanation

Discussion presents arguments for and against, or several factors, and usually ends with a reasoned conclusion.

Example

Discuss the causes of inflation.

In an exam

Command-word meanings can vary between subjects, exam boards and mark schemes, so check your own board's guidance. See Exam Command Words: What Each Instruction Asks For.

Common confusion

Discuss is not a list. Each point needs development.

Related terms

  • EvaluateWeigh strengths and limitations and reach a supported judgment.
  • AssessJudge the importance, quality or extent of something, weighing evidence.
  • ExamineLook closely at something and investigate its parts or details.
