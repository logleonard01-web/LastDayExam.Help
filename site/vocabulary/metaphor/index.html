Metaphor

noun · English

Definition

A figure of speech that describes one thing as if it were another, without like or as.

Explanation

Metaphors create vivid images and compress comparison.

Example

Time is a thief.

Common confusion

A simile uses like or as; a metaphor does not.

Related terms

  • SimileA figure of speech that compares two things using like or as.
