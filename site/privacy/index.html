Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21.

What we collect

This site does not require accounts and does not ask you to submit personal information to read content. Search runs in your browser against a file hosted with the site; your search terms are not sent to a separate search service.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

The site itself does not set tracking cookies. If we add analytics or advertising in future, we will update this policy, describe the services used and provide any consent choices required by law.
