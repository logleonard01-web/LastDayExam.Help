About

Last Day Exam Help helps students understand academic concepts, prepare for exams and learn the vocabulary they need, especially when study time is limited.

Our mission is to make difficult concepts easier to understand. We publish study guides, a growing academic vocabulary library, quick-revision guides and original practice questions.

What we do not claim

We do not promise exam results, and we do not suggest that a subject can be mastered overnight. Last-minute revision is a way to review, organise and strengthen knowledge you already have.

Who it is for

Students preparing for exams, plus teachers, parents, homeschoolers and independent learners.
