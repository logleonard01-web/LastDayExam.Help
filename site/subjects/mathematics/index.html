Key vocabulary

  • AreaThe amount of surface a 2D shape covers.
  • CoefficientA number that multiplies a variable.
  • EquationA statement that two expressions are equal.
  • EventA set of one or more outcomes in probability.
  • ExpressionA combination of numbers, variables and operations without an equals sign.
  • FactorA number or expression that divides another exactly.
  • FractionA number that represents part of a whole, written as one number over another.
  • HypotenuseThe longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle.
  • InequalityA statement that compares two expressions using less than, greater than or similar signs.
  • MeanThe sum of all values divided by the number of values.
  • MedianThe middle value when data is arranged in order.
  • ModeThe most frequent value in a data set.
  • OutcomeOne possible result of a probability experiment.
  • PercentageA fraction expressed out of 100.
  • PerimeterThe total distance around the outside of a 2D shape.
  • ProbabilityA number from 0 to 1 that measures how likely an event is.
  • Quadratic equationAn equation that can be written as ax squared plus bx plus c equals 0, where a is not zero.
  • RadiusThe distance from the centre of a circle to its edge.
  • RangeThe difference between the largest and smallest values.
  • RatioA comparison of two quantities showing how many times one contains the other.
  • Right angleAn angle of exactly 90 degrees.
  • RootA solution of an equation, or a number that when multiplied by itself a given number of times gives a value.
  • VariableA symbol, often a letter, representing a quantity that can change or is unknown.
  • VolumeThe amount of space inside a 3D object.
