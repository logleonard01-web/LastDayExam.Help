Key vocabulary

  • BiasA tendency to present or favour one point of view unfairly.
  • CitationA reference in your text that shows where an idea, quotation or fact came from.
  • CredibilityThe degree to which a source can be trusted.
  • EvidenceFacts, data, examples or sources used to support a claim.
  • ParaphraseRestate an idea from a source in your own words and sentence structure.
  • PlagiarismPresenting someone else's words or ideas as your own.
