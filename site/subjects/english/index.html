Key vocabulary

  • Affect vs effectAffect is usually a verb meaning to influence; effect is usually a noun meaning a result.
  • ArgumentA claim supported by reasons and evidence.
  • Its vs it'sIts shows possession; it's means it is or it has.
  • MetaphorA figure of speech that describes one thing as if it were another, without like or as.
  • SimileA figure of speech that compares two things using like or as.
  • Their, there, they'reTheir shows possession, there shows place or existence, and they're means they are.
  • Then vs thanThen refers to time or sequence; than is used in comparisons.
  • Thesis statementA sentence that states the central, arguable claim of an essay.
  • Topic sentenceA sentence that states the main idea of a paragraph.
