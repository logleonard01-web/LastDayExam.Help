Key vocabulary

  • AccelerationThe rate of change of velocity.
  • AcidA substance that produces hydrogen ions in water and has a pH below 7.
  • BaseA substance that neutralises acids.
  • CellThe basic unit of structure and function in living organisms.
  • Chemical equationA representation of a chemical reaction using formulas.
  • ChlorophyllThe green pigment in plants that absorbs light for photosynthesis.
  • ChloroplastAn organelle in plant and algal cells that contains chlorophyll and carries out photosynthesis.
  • Dependent variableThe variable you measure in an experiment.
  • DisplacementThe straight-line distance and direction from the start to the end point.
  • ForceA push or pull on an object, measured in newtons.
  • GlucoseA simple sugar that cells use as a source of energy.
  • HypothesisA testable explanation or prediction that evidence can support or contradict.
  • Independent variableThe variable you deliberately change in an experiment.
  • InertiaThe tendency of an object to resist changes in its motion.
  • MassThe amount of matter in an object, measured in kilograms.
  • MitochondrionAn organelle where aerobic respiration releases energy for the cell.
  • NeutralisationThe reaction of an acid with a base to form a salt and water.
  • OrganelleA structure inside a cell that has a specific function.
  • pHA scale, usually from 0 to 14, that measures how acidic or alkaline a solution is.
  • PhotosynthesisThe process by which plants and some other organisms use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water.
  • ProductA substance formed in a chemical reaction.
  • ReactantA substance that is present at the start of a chemical reaction and is changed by it.
  • VelocitySpeed in a given direction.
