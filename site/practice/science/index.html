Science Practice Questions

10 questions. Work each one out before revealing the answer.

Question 1

Science · Photosynthesis · Easy

Which gas is released as a product of photosynthesis?

  • A. Carbon dioxide
  • B. Oxygen
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Hydrogen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Oxygen

Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide and water to make glucose and oxygen.

Common misconception: Mixing up the inputs and outputs of photosynthesis and respiration.

Question 2

Science · Forces · Easy

A resultant force of 18 N acts on a 6 kg object. What is its acceleration?

  • A. 0.33 m/s²
  • B. 3 m/s²
  • C. 12 m/s²
  • D. 108 m/s²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3 m/s²

Use F = ma, so a = F ÷ m = 18 ÷ 6 = 3 m/s².

Common misconception: Multiplying force by mass instead of dividing.

Question 3

Science · Speed · Easy

A runner covers 150 m in 30 s. What is the average speed?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 5 m/s

Speed = distance ÷ time = 150 ÷ 30 = 5 m/s.

Common misconception: Dividing time by distance.

Question 4

Science · Balancing equations · Medium

Which is the correctly balanced equation for magnesium burning in oxygen?

  • A. Mg + O2 → MgO
  • B. 2Mg + O2 → 2MgO
  • C. Mg + O2 → MgO2
  • D. 2Mg + 2O2 → 2MgO
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 2Mg + O2 → 2MgO

Each side has 2 Mg and 2 O in 2Mg + O2 → 2MgO. The formula MgO must not be changed to balance.

Common misconception: Changing the formula (MgO2) rather than the coefficient.

Question 5

Science · pH · Medium

How many times greater is the hydrogen ion concentration in a solution of pH 2 than in a solution of pH 4?

  • A. 2
  • B. 10
  • C. 100
  • D. 1000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 100

Each pH unit is a factor of 10. Two units gives 10 × 10 = 100.

Common misconception: Treating the pH scale as linear.

Question 6

Science · Cells · Easy

Which organelle is the main site of aerobic respiration?

  • A. Nucleus
  • B. Mitochondrion
  • C. Ribosome
  • D. Cell wall
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Mitochondrion

Mitochondria release energy from glucose using oxygen.

Common misconception: Saying the nucleus provides energy.

Question 7

Science · Experiments · Easy

An experiment investigates how temperature affects the rate of an enzyme reaction. What is the independent variable?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: Temperature

The independent variable is the factor deliberately changed. The rate of reaction is the dependent variable.

Common misconception: Confusing independent and dependent variables.

Question 8

Science · Acceleration · Medium

A car increases its speed from 0 to 20 m/s in 5 s. What is its acceleration?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 4 m/s²

a = (v − u) ÷ t = (20 − 0) ÷ 5 = 4 m/s².

Common misconception: Forgetting to subtract the initial velocity.

Question 9

Science · Weight · Medium

Using g = 10 N/kg, what is the weight of a 5 kg object?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 50 N

W = m × g = 5 × 10 = 50 N.

Common misconception: Confusing weight with mass.

Question 10

Science · Cells · Easy

Which type of cell has a cell wall made of cellulose?

  • A. Animal cell
  • B. Plant cell
  • C. Human red blood cell
  • D. Bacterial cell
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Plant cell

Plant cell walls are made of cellulose. Bacterial cell walls have a different composition.

Common misconception: Saying bacteria have cellulose walls.
