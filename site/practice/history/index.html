History and Geography Practice Questions

5 questions. Work each one out before revealing the answer.

Question 1

History and Geography · Sources · Easy

A soldier's diary written during a war is best described as which type of source?

  • A. Primary
  • B. Secondary
  • C. Fictional
  • D. Textbook
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Primary

It was written at the time by someone who experienced events first-hand.

Common misconception: Assuming a diary must be reliable because it is primary.

Question 2

History and Geography · Geography · Easy

What is the latitude of the equator?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 0 degrees

The equator is the line of latitude at 0 degrees.

Common misconception: Confusing latitude and longitude.

Question 3

History and Geography · Sources · Easy

A textbook published in 2015 about the Roman Empire is which type of source for Roman history?

  • A. Primary
  • B. Secondary
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Secondary

It was written long after the events and interprets earlier evidence.

Common misconception: Thinking all old books are primary.

Question 4

History and Geography · Evaluating sources · Medium

True or false: a biased source cannot be useful to historians.

  • A. True
  • B. False
Show answer and explanation

Answer: False

A biased source can still show what the author or group believed or wanted others to believe.

Common misconception: Rejecting a source only because it is biased.

Question 5

History and Geography · Geography · Easy

Where do lines of longitude meet?

  • A. At the equator
  • B. At the poles
  • C. At the prime meridian
  • D. They never meet
Show answer and explanation

Answer: At the poles

Meridians run from the North Pole to the South Pole and meet at both.

Common misconception: Assuming lines of longitude are parallel.
