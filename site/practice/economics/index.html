Economics Practice Questions

5 questions. Work each one out before revealing the answer.

Question 1

Economics · Supply and demand · Easy

If the price of a good is set above its equilibrium price, what is likely to happen?

  • A. A shortage
  • B. A surplus
  • C. Demand shifts right
  • D. Supply shifts left
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A surplus

At a price above equilibrium, quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded.

Common misconception: Mixing up shortage and surplus.

Question 2

Economics · Opportunity cost · Medium

A bakery with one oven can bake bread worth 400 or cakes worth 500 in a day, not both. It bakes cakes. What is the opportunity cost?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 400 (the value of the bread given up)

Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative forgone, which is the bread.

Common misconception: Saying the opportunity cost is 500 or 900.

Question 3

Economics · Inflation · Medium

A price index rises from 250 to 260 in a year. What is the inflation rate?

  • A. 2%
  • B. 4%
  • C. 10%
  • D. 26%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 4%

The change is 10, and 10 ÷ 250 × 100 = 4%.

Common misconception: Dividing by the new value.

Question 4

Economics · Supply and demand · Easy

If demand for a good increases and supply stays the same, what happens to the equilibrium price?

  • A. It falls
  • B. It rises
  • C. It stays the same
  • D. It cannot be known
Show answer and explanation

Answer: It rises

The demand curve shifts right, so the equilibrium price and quantity increase.

Common misconception: Moving the wrong curve.

Question 5

Economics · Supply and demand · Medium

A frost damages a fruit crop. What happens to the equilibrium price and quantity of the fruit?

  • A. Price rises, quantity falls
  • B. Price falls, quantity rises
  • C. Both rise
  • D. Both fall
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Price rises, quantity falls

Supply shifts left, which raises the price and reduces the quantity traded.

Common misconception: Confusing a shift with a movement along a curve.
