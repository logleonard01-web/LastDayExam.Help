English Practice Questions

6 questions. Work each one out before revealing the answer.

Question 1

English · Figurative language · Easy

Is "The classroom was a zoo" a metaphor or a simile?

  • A. Metaphor
  • B. Simile
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Metaphor

It says the classroom was a zoo without using like or as.

Common misconception: Thinking any comparison is a simile.

Question 2

English · Confused words · Easy

Choose the correct word: "The weather will ___ our plans." (affect/effect)

  • A. affect
  • B. effect
Show answer and explanation

Answer: affect

Here a verb meaning to influence is needed, and affect is the usual verb.

Common misconception: Using effect as the verb.

Question 3

English · Thesis statements · Medium

Which of these is the strongest thesis statement for an essay?

  • A. Pollution is bad.
  • B. This essay is about pollution.
  • C. City governments should limit private car use because it reduces air pollution more cost-effectively than other measures.
  • D. Many people think about pollution.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: City governments should limit private car use because it reduces air pollution more cost-effectively than other measures.

The strongest thesis is specific and arguable and gives a reason. The others are too general or merely announce a topic.

Common misconception: Writing a topic or a fact instead of a claim.

Question 4

English · Confused words · Easy

Complete the sentence: "The cat licked ___ paws." (its/it's)

  • A. its
  • B. it's
Show answer and explanation

Answer: its

Its shows possession. It's would mean it is or it has.

Common misconception: Adding an apostrophe to show possession.

Question 5

English · Citing sources · Medium

Why does a paraphrase of someone else's idea still need a citation?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: The idea belongs to the original author, even if the words are yours.

Citations credit ideas as well as exact words.

Common misconception: Believing that changing the wording removes the need to cite.

Question 6

English · Figurative language · Easy

Which sentence contains a simile?

  • A. Time is a thief.
  • B. She was as brave as a lion.
  • C. The wind whispered.
  • D. Life is a journey.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: She was as brave as a lion.

A simile compares using like or as.

Common misconception: Confusing similes with metaphors.
