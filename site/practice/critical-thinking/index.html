Critical Thinking Practice Questions

3 questions. Work each one out before revealing the answer.

Question 1

Critical Thinking · Fallacies · Easy

"Either you support this policy or you hate your country." Which fallacy is this?

  • A. Straw man
  • B. False dilemma
  • C. Circular reasoning
  • D. Slippery slope
Show answer and explanation

Answer: False dilemma

It presents only two options when others exist.

Common misconception: Confusing false dilemma with slippery slope.

Question 2

Critical Thinking · Correlation · Medium

Ice cream sales and sunburn cases both rise in summer. What is the best explanation?

  • A. Ice cream causes sunburn
  • B. Sunburn causes ice cream sales
  • C. Hot, sunny weather affects both
  • D. It is impossible to explain
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Hot, sunny weather affects both

A third factor, the weather, increases both, so the correlation does not show that one causes the other.

Common misconception: Assuming correlation shows causation.

Question 3

Critical Thinking · Fallacies · Easy

"You can't trust her view on tax; she's rich." Which fallacy is this?

  • A. Ad hominem
  • B. Appeal to authority
  • C. Hasty generalisation
  • D. Post hoc
Show answer and explanation

Answer: Ad hominem

It attacks the person instead of the argument.

Common misconception: Thinking any criticism of a person is a fallacy; it depends on whether it addresses the argument.
