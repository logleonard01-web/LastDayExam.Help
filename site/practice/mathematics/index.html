Mathematics Practice Questions

12 questions. Work each one out before revealing the answer.

Question 1

Mathematics · Linear equations · Easy

Solve for x: 3x + 5 = 20.

Show answer and explanation

Answer: x = 5

Subtract 5 from both sides to get 3x = 15, then divide both sides by 3.

Common misconception: Adding 5 instead of subtracting it from both sides.

Question 2

Mathematics · Percentages · Easy

What is 40% of 250?

  • A. 75
  • B. 100
  • C. 110
  • D. 125
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 100

40% is 0.4. Then 0.4 × 250 = 100. Alternatively, 10% is 25, so 40% is 4 × 25 = 100.

Common misconception: Dividing by 40 instead of multiplying by 0.4.

Question 3

Mathematics · Averages · Easy

What is the mean of 6, 8, 10 and 12?

  • A. 8
  • B. 9
  • C. 10
  • D. 36
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 9

The sum is 36 and there are four values, so the mean is 36 ÷ 4 = 9.

Common misconception: Forgetting to divide the sum by the number of values.

Question 4

Mathematics · Averages · Easy

Find the median of 3, 9, 5, 7 and 1.

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 5

Order the values: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. The middle value is 5.

Common misconception: Taking the middle value without ordering the data first.

Question 5

Mathematics · Area · Easy

A triangle has a base of 10 cm and a perpendicular height of 6 cm. What is its area?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 30 cm²

Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 cm².

Common misconception: Forgetting the half.

Question 6

Mathematics · Pythagoras · Medium

A right-angled triangle has shorter sides of 9 cm and 12 cm. How long is the hypotenuse?

  • A. 13 cm
  • B. 15 cm
  • C. 17 cm
  • D. 21 cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 15 cm

c² = 9² + 12² = 81 + 144 = 225, so c = 15 cm.

Common misconception: Adding the sides (9 + 12) instead of squaring first.

Question 7

Mathematics · Quadratic equations · Medium

Solve x² − 7x + 12 = 0.

Show answer and explanation

Answer: x = 3 or x = 4

Two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to −7 are −3 and −4, so (x − 3)(x − 4) = 0. Check: 9 − 21 + 12 = 0 and 16 − 28 + 12 = 0.

Common misconception: Giving only one solution.

Question 8

Mathematics · Probability · Medium

A bag contains 4 red and 6 blue counters. One is picked at random. What is the probability it is red?

  • A. 2/5
  • B. 4/6
  • C. 3/5
  • D. 1/4
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 2/5

There are 10 counters and 4 are red, so the probability is 4/10 = 2/5.

Common misconception: Comparing red to blue (4/6) instead of red to total.

Question 9

Mathematics · Percentage change · Medium

A price rises from 50 to 65. What is the percentage increase?

  • A. 15%
  • B. 30%
  • C. 23%
  • D. 130%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 30%

The increase is 15. Then 15 ÷ 50 × 100 = 30%.

Common misconception: Dividing by the new price instead of the original.

Question 10

Mathematics · Inequalities · Medium

Solve −3x < 12.

Show answer and explanation

Answer: x > −4

Divide both sides by −3 and reverse the inequality sign, giving x > −4. Check: x = 0 gives 0 < 12, which is true.

Common misconception: Forgetting to reverse the sign when dividing by a negative.

Question 11

Mathematics · Circles · Medium

A circle has radius 4 cm. Using π = 3.14, what is its area?

  • A. 25.12 cm²
  • B. 50.24 cm²
  • C. 12.56 cm²
  • D. 200.96 cm²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 50.24 cm²

Area = πr² = 3.14 × 16 = 50.24 cm².

Common misconception: Using 2πr (circumference) or using the diameter as the radius.

Question 12

Mathematics · Fractions · Easy

Write 3/8 as a decimal.

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 0.375

Divide the numerator by the denominator: 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375.

Common misconception: Dividing 8 by 3.
